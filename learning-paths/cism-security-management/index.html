CISM Security Management Study Lab

An original, scenario-driven learning companion for security managers. Learn the concepts, make decisions in safe simulations, then practice management-focused questions.

Independent educational resource. Not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by ISACA. This is not an official CISM course or a guarantee of exam readiness.

How to study

  1. Read the official CISM exam content outline for the current exam version.
  2. Study one domain using the free readings and video discovery links.
  3. Complete the domain's decision simulation before opening its practice questions.
  4. Review the rationale for both correct and incorrect options.
  5. Repeat weaker domains and use authorized exam preparation resources to validate readiness.
